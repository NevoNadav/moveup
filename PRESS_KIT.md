## Short product description

MoveUp is a free, ad-free iPhone app for public transport in Israel. Created by Nevo Nadav, it brings bus and train planning, walking connections, available live arrivals and journey guidance into one experience in Hebrew and English.

## Extended product description

MoveUp helps people understand the whole public transport journey: how to reach the first stop, which service to take, what happens during a transfer and when to prepare to get off. Passengers can compare routes, choose a departure or arrival time, explore lines and follow guidance on a map. Live arrival estimates and bus positions appear when suitable reports are available, with scheduled information distinguished from live data.

The app includes optional spoken prompts, stop reminders, Live Activities, widgets and community features. It is an independent project created and developed by Nevo Nadav. MoveUp is free and has no ads; it does not sell or validate transport tickets.
