[FAQ](FAQ.md) · [שאלות נפוצות](he/FAQ.md) · [Official website](https://moveup.org.il/)

## Report a product issue

Describe what you were trying to do, what happened and what you expected. Include the app version and iOS version if you know them. For a transport-data problem, a public line number, direction and stop name or number can help.

If sharing a screenshot, first check for private information. Do not publish a home address, email address, precise personal location, account identifier, private sharing link or authentication information. Use email for enquiries that should remain private.

Public GitHub issues are suitable for general product feedback and errors in these public guides. They are visible to everyone. Do not use them to send personal trip histories.

## Suggest an improvement

Explain the moment in your journey that is difficult, and what would make it easier. A concrete example such as “I could not tell which direction to board” is more useful than a proposed feature name alone.

## Account and privacy requests

See the official [account-deletion page](https://moveup.org.il/en/delete-account/) and [privacy policy](https://moveup.org.il/privacy-en). Use the contact details and processes on those pages for personal requests.

## Press and collaboration
