## Your choices in the app

Basic planning and navigation are available as a guest. Features involving location, accounts, sharing or photo contributions have different data needs. Review the official policy for the details relevant to the features you use and the permissions you grant.

## This public showcase

The repository contains public editorial material and selected product images. It is not a place to submit account information, private journey links or personal location history. GitHub issues and comments are public.

For privacy questions or personal requests, contact [moveup.israel@gmail.com](mailto:moveup.israel@gmail.com) through the process described in the official policy.
