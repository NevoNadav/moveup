# How to plan a bus and train journey in Israel with MoveUp

[עברית](../he/plan-a-journey.md) · [MoveUp](../README.md)

The useful question is not just “Which bus?” It is “What does the whole journey look like?” MoveUp helps you compare the walk, the service and any changes before you start.

## Choose a clear start and destination

Enter the place you want to reach. You can plan from your current location when permission is enabled, or select a starting point manually. When several places have similar names, check the map and the destination before continuing.

## Choose the right time and date

Use “leave now” for an immediate trip, a later departure for planning ahead, or “arrive by” for an appointment. Read the service date as well as the time: a result after midnight may belong to the next calendar day. Service patterns can vary on weekends and holidays.

## Compare complete journeys

Look at these details together:

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Total duration | Includes more than time spent on the bus or train. |
| Walk to the first stop | Determines when you actually need to leave. |
| Number of changes | More connections create more opportunities for delay. |
| Walk during a transfer | Reduces the time available to catch the next service. |
| Final walk | The arrival stop may not be the destination itself. |

A few minutes saved on the timetable may be less useful than a connection with comfortable walking time. Choose the option that suits your priorities.

## Check before boarding

Confirm the line number, direction and destination shown on the vehicle or platform. Recheck the departure information when you reach the stop. If the app shows a live estimate, remember that it can move as conditions change.

## Follow the next step

Start guidance and follow the route. Optional voice prompts and reminders can support the journey, while the map and next-step information help you stay oriented. Use manual controls if automatic progress needs correcting.

For route information that matters to accessibility, verify the actual station and vehicle conditions with the operator. MoveUp cannot guarantee every lift, path or vehicle is accessible.
