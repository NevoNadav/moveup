# Nevo Nadav — creator and developer of MoveUp

**נבו נדב · Independent app developer · Creator of MoveUp**

[Official profile](https://moveup.org.il/en/nevo-nadav/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/NevoNadav) · [עברית](he/CREATOR.md)

Nevo Nadav is the independent creator and developer of MoveUp, a free, ad-free public transport navigation app for Israel. MoveUp is available for iPhone in Hebrew and English.

## The work behind MoveUp

MoveUp brings route planning, line search, available live arrivals, walking connections and journey guidance into a single passenger experience. Nevo's focus is practical: help a person understand where to go next and how much confidence to place in the information on screen.

That focus appears in the distinction between live and scheduled times, the inclusion of walking in transfer decisions and the ability to follow a journey through iPhone Live Activities. Hebrew and English are part of the product experience, alongside light and dark appearances.

## Independent products, useful everyday tools

Nevo's work also includes **Reshit (ראשית)**, a Jewish app from the Nadav family. His [official creator page](https://moveup.org.il/en/nevo-nadav/) introduces both projects and links to their official sites.

MoveUp develops with passenger feedback: reports about confusing journeys, suggestions for clearer guidance and ideas that make regular trips easier. Its community features include crowding reports and reviewed station photos.
