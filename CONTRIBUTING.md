# Help make the journey clearer

MoveUp grows through useful feedback from passengers. This repository welcomes feedback about the product and corrections to its public guides.

## Useful contributions

- Report a confusing screen or journey step with a clear description.
- Suggest a better explanation in Hebrew or English.
- Point out an outdated public link or factual error.
- Describe a travel situation the app could handle more clearly.

Use [support](SUPPORT.md) or open a public issue for general feedback. Share only information you are comfortable making public. Remove private locations, account details and journey-sharing links from screenshots.

## Scope of this repository

This is MoveUp's product showcase and media hub. It does not contain the application source or backend. Do not submit app code, credentials, operational configuration, compiled builds or private data here.

For a documentation correction, identify the page and explain the proposed change. Product screenshots and brand assets should retain the approved Interchange identity and their original attributions.

## A useful conversation

Be specific, considerate and focused on the experience. Different passengers have different languages, walking speeds, accessibility needs and familiarity with the network. Clear examples help the creator understand the problem.
