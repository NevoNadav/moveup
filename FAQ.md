# MoveUp: frequently asked questions

[עברית](he/FAQ.md) · [Product overview](README.md)

## What is MoveUp?

MoveUp is an independent public transport navigation app for Israel, created by Nevo Nadav. It combines bus and train journey planning, walking directions, available live arrival information and guidance during a trip.

## Is MoveUp free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no advertising. Public transport fares are separate: you still need to pay and validate your journey through an authorized method.

## Where can I download it?

For iPhone, use the [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/il/app/moveup-israel-transit/id6834863987). MoveUp requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Check the listing for current regional availability and compatibility.

## Is there an Android version?

Yes. Download the released Android app from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nevonadav.moveup). It supports English and Hebrew. The store shows device and regional compatibility; Live Updates require a supported device and Android version. This showcase does not distribute APK files.

## Does it support English?

Yes. MoveUp supports English and Hebrew, including Hebrew right-to-left layouts. The interface offers light and dark appearances.

## Do I need an account?

You can plan routes and navigate as a guest. Some community features, including contributing station photos, require sign-in.

## Is every arrival time live?

No. Live arrival estimates depend on fresh operator reports. When live information is unavailable, the app distinguishes the scheduled information it can show. Estimates can change. See [live versus scheduled information](guides/live-arrivals.md).

## Does a missing bus marker mean the service is cancelled?

No. It can mean that no usable vehicle report is available. Check the departure information and operator notices; absence of a marker is not proof of cancellation.

## Can I choose when to leave or arrive?

Yes. The planner supports leaving now, departing later and arriving by a selected time. Check the actual service date, especially when planning late at night or across days with limited service.

## Can I buy or validate tickets?

No. The Tickets area provides fare information and a preview of a possible future service. It does not issue tickets, take payments or validate journeys.

## Does the app work entirely offline?

No. Route calculation and fresh arrival information need a connection. Saved information may be useful as a reference, but it is not a substitute for updated service information.

## Does MoveUp guarantee accessible journeys?

No. Accessibility depends on the available map and transport information and actual conditions. Confirm important station, lift and vehicle requirements with the operator before travelling.

## Who created MoveUp?

[Nevo Nadav (נבו נדב)](CREATOR.md), an independent app developer. MoveUp is not an official Ministry of Transport or operator service.

## Where is the application code?

This repository is a product showcase containing public information and media. It does not distribute the application's source, backend, credentials or compiled builds.

## How can I report a problem?

See [support](SUPPORT.md). For private enquiries, write to [moveup.israel@gmail.com](mailto:moveup.israel@gmail.com). Do not post private journey-sharing links, account details or precise personal locations in a public issue.
