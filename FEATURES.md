# Inside MoveUp

[Download for iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/il/app/moveup-israel-transit/id6834863987) · [עברית](he/FEATURES.md)

MoveUp connects the decisions before a trip with the guidance you need during it. Here is how its main features fit into an everyday journey.

## Before leaving

Search for a destination and compare journeys that combine bus, train and walking. Choose between leaving now, departing later and arriving by a particular time. Look at the total duration, the number of changes and how much walking each option includes.

A shorter scheduled trip is not automatically the most comfortable one. A route with fewer changes or a longer connection can be more suitable for a particular day. Travel preferences help make those choices visible.

## Finding the right service

Search lines, inspect their directions and stops, and open departure information. Check the destination as well as the line number: the same line may run in different directions.

When fresh operator reports are available, MoveUp can display live bus positions and arrival estimates. Scheduled information remains identifiable when a live report is unavailable. A marker is an observation of a vehicle; it is not a guarantee of when that vehicle will reach you.

## During the journey

Map guidance shows the journey and next step. Optional spoken prompts and stop reminders can help you follow progress. Live Activities and widgets bring selected journey information onto iPhone system surfaces. Availability and presentation depend on the device, settings and active journey.

Location-based progress can move a journey forward automatically, with manual controls when a correction is needed. Always check the actual stop, direction and surroundings when travelling.

## Transfers that include the walk

The time between two services is not all waiting time: part of it may be spent walking to another stop. MoveUp includes that connection walk in its guidance so a tight change is easier to understand before choosing it.

See [our transfer example](guides/transfers.md) for the difference between a seven-minute timetable gap and the time actually left after walking.

## Familiar trips and community contributions

Favorite places and recent searches make repeated trips easier to start. Optional community features include sharing a journey with trusted people, reporting crowding and contributing station photos. Photo contributions require sign-in and are reviewed before publication.

## Language and appearance

Hebrew and English, light and dark appearances, readable text and a consistent visual identity are part of the experience. Location permission is useful for location-based guidance; you can choose your starting point manually when planning.

## What MoveUp does not do

MoveUp does not sell, pay for or validate transport tickets. Its fare information does not replace an authorized payment method. It cannot guarantee a connection, a live report for every service or the accessibility of every vehicle and station.
